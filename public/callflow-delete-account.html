CallFlow Privacy Policy

Request account or data deletion

You can request deletion without signing into CallFlow.

  1. Email Yogeshkukadiya92@gmail.com with the subject “CallFlow account and data deletion request”.
  2. Include your registered email or mobile, organization/CRM name, and whether you want account deletion, associated personal data deletion, or specific records corrected/deleted.
  3. Support will verify your identity, coordinate with your CRM administrator, and explain the scope, expected processing time and any records that must be retained.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, OTPs or copies of other people's call logs. If you cannot use email, call +91 9825344428 for help.

What the request covers

Requests may cover account/profile information, registered device information, call metadata, notes, location check-ins and follow-up records associated with your account. Shared business lead records and records subject to legal retention may require your organization's review rather than automatic deletion. Support will explain any retained categories and applicable retention period.

What happens next

This is a manual support request, not an automatic deletion form. Opening the email link does not submit a request; you must send the email. Deleting the app removes neither your CRM account nor already synchronized records. Support will confirm the outcome after processing.
